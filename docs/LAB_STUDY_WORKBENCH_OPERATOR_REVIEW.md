# Broader Study-first Lab operator review

**Status:** Directional checkpoint accepted on 2026-10-14. The broader layout and Study-first doorway may carry forward. This is not final approval of the mature Lab, exact controls or native implementation.

Read with the [interaction specification](LAB_STUDY_WORKBENCH_INTERACTION_SPEC.md), [prototype](prototypes/lab-study-workbench-v0.html), [Cockpit UX implementation plan](COCKPIT_UX_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) and [isolated AMDX Lab review](LAB_AMDX_CALENDAR_TREE_OPERATOR_REVIEW.md).

## Operator report

The operator broadly likes the layout and has no specific immediate changes. **Study this competency** placed front and center at Lab startup feels like a useful on-ramp into Study. The first inspection produced no immediate feelings of friction.

Some acclimation is expected because the interface and available functions are new. The operator wants to revisit the composition when a fuller integrated experience supports a real test Study session, Replay and related routes. Adjustments are expected as the whole suite matures.

## Direction that carries forward

- Lab may open with a prominent but dismissible **Study this competency** recommendation.
- The broad Study map / central workspace / contextual support-rail composition is promising enough to stop isolated layout polishing.
- Study Plan, Evidence, Criteria, Samples and AMDX Calendar may continue as coordinated lenses around one selected competency/question.
- AMDX remains foundational and readily reachable without becoming the whole identity of Lab.
- A constrained contextual rail and recoverable Study Find remain appropriate hypotheses for integrated implementation.
- No concrete first-use friction requires another standalone-prototype revision.

## Acceptance boundary

This review accepts the **direction**, not the finished experience. It does not yet establish:

- final controls, labels, geometry, density, styling or navigation taxonomy;
- repeated-use efficiency or learned muscle memory;
- real database, screenshot, Trade Plan revision or evidence behavior;
- complete Historical Backtest, Replay, Forward or Review integration;
- final AMDX temporal zoom, calendar editing or nested-context rendering;
- native input, macro-pad/CAD-mouse mappings or monitor arrangements;
- readiness, eligibility, classifier or broker behavior.

The decisive later evaluation is a realistic end-to-end session: start from the recommendation, establish or resume a question, inspect context, gather chart evidence, capture a Study Find, run a Study/Replay operation, return through Review and reacquire the original context. That evaluation should judge workflow friction and cognitive continuity, not only isolated screens.
